MoodTune Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

MoodTune estimates a mood from information on your iPhone and can open a Spotify playlist for that mood. This policy describes what stays on your device and what is sent to Spotify. MoodTune does not operate its own account system or data server.

Health data

If you allow it, MoodTune reads these categories from Apple Health: heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, steps, active energy, workouts, and mindfulness minutes. The app uses those readings only on your device to estimate a mood.

MoodTune does not write Health data. It does not upload Health data to the developer. If Health has no readings, the app can show labeled sample figures stored in the app itself. Those figures are not your Health data.

Chat

Messages you type are saved on your iPhone so the conversation and mood estimate remain when you reopen the app. They are not sent to the developer.

Spotify

Connecting Spotify is optional. If you connect, MoodTune stores the Spotify Client ID you enter on your iPhone and stores the sign-in tokens in the iOS Keychain. The app then contacts Spotify to sign you in, read your Spotify display name, search for playlists, and, when possible, start playback.

Those requests go to Spotify, not to a MoodTune server. Spotify’s own policy applies to data Spotify receives: spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy.

You can also open a playlist in the Spotify app or on the Spotify website without connecting an account. In that case, Spotify receives the page or playlist request from your device.

What MoodTune does not do

  • MoodTune does not sell personal information.
  • MoodTune does not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • MoodTune does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • MoodTune does not use the data above to train a model.

Children

MoodTune is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting information

Disconnect Spotify in MoodTune settings to remove the saved Spotify session from this iPhone. Delete MoodTune to remove the chat, the saved Client ID, and other preferences stored by the app. Health samples remain in Apple Health because MoodTune never copies them off the device or writes them.

Changes
